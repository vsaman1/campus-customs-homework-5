# Problem 8 - Agent Dashboard Design

The dashboard is an editorial operations desk rather than a generic dashboard. A warm paper field and serif display typography make the work surface feel deliberate, while deep campus green identifies active agent work.

- **Layout:** A fixed ticket rail supports rapid switching between the three requests. The central workbench keeps the selected request, its controls, the agent relay, summaries, and any approval action in one scanning path.
- **Agent activity:** The dark-green relay is visually distinct from management controls. Events identify the agent, action, tool result or outcome, and time, so live automation reads like an auditable operations log.
- **Ticket state:** Open items use gold status dots. Resolved items use green state markers and are disabled from rerunning. The ticket rail retains all work for comparison.
- **Cash and approval:** Checking balance is always visible in the header. Prepared payments have a separate gold review panel, intentionally apart from the ordinary run control. A ticket with a payment proposal pauses after the agent run; only the human approval button can spend cash, after which the ticket is resolved.
- **Responsive behavior:** The rail becomes a horizontally scrollable ticket strip on narrow screens, and the relay and approval surfaces stack without losing their reading order.
